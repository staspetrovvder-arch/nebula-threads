05 / FAQ

Questions from
mission control.

How small can the run be?

Starter runs are designed for small teams and first launches. Tell us your headcount and we will shape the production route around it.

Can you work from an existing logo?

Yes. A logo is useful as a signal, but the artwork does not have to become a logo treatment. We can build a wider visual language around it.

How long does a drop take?

Simple productions can move in a week; more art-directed drops need time for sampling and refinement. The quoted timeline is set around your launch date.

Do you ship internationally?

International fulfilment can be arranged for qualifying drops. Shipping, taxes and destination constraints are confirmed with the project quote.
